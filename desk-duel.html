How to play

1Grab & pull back

Press on your pen and drag away from where you want it to go, like a slingshot. Pull further for more power.

2Let go to flick

Your pen slides, spins and collides with real physics. The dotted line predicts your path.

3Add spin

Grab near the end of your pen to make it spin. Spinning pens sweep a wider area and curve as they slide.

4Knock them off

A pen falls when its center dot leaves the desk. Last pen standing wins the round.

5Weight matters

Heavy pens hit harder and are tough to shove, but slide shorter and need precise shots. Heavier classes face tougher rivals.

6Earn & upgrade

Win matches, land knockouts and trick shots to earn coins. Spend them on pens and desks in the shop.

Rules

  • Players take turns, one flick each. In Party mode, play rotates between all players.
  • Knock rivals off and stay on yourself. If your own pen falls, you are out for the round.
  • If every remaining pen falls on the same flick, the round is replayed.
  • First to the target number of round wins takes the match.
  • With a shot clock, a turn is skipped when time runs out.
  • Online: one player taps Online, then Create a room and shares the 5-letter code. Friends tap Online and type it in. Up to 3 players; the host can fill a seat with a CPU.

Trick shots (bonus coins)

  • Double KO: knock two rivals off with one flick.
  • Bank shot: bounce off an obstacle before the knockout.
  • Spin KO: knock out a rival with a fast-spinning pen.
  • Sniper: knock out a rival from long range.
  • Chain KO: your pen hits a rival who knocks out another.
  • Close call: your pen stops within 1 cm of the edge.

Controls

  • Touch or mouse: press on your pen, drag back, release.
  • Drag back to the pen to cancel a shot. Esc pauses. Space continues after a round.
