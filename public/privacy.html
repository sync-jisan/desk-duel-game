Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026 · Applies to the Pen Fight Android app and the Pen Fight website.

Summary

  • You can play with an offline profile that never leaves your device, or sign in with Google to save progress to the cloud and play online.
  • We collect only what the game needs: your Google name, email and profile photo, and your game progress.
  • No ads. We never sell your data. No third-party analytics or tracking.
  • You can delete your account at any time in Settings → Delete account.

1. Data we collect

When you sign in with Google

Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth using Google. We receive from Google:

  • Name (used as your starting gamer name, which you can change)
  • Email address (used only to identify your account; never shown to other players)
  • Profile photo (optional; shown as your avatar)
  • A unique account ID that links your progress to your sign-in

We never see or store your Google password.

Game progress (signed-in players)

  • Gamer name, avatar colour, equipped pen
  • Coins, XP, level, trophies, pens and desks you own, career progress, achievements
  • Match statistics (wins, losses, knockouts, streaks), daily login streak and last time online

Online play

  • Rooms: room code, host account ID, the players in the lobby (names, pens, colours) and room settings. Rooms are temporary and are deleted automatically (normally within minutes after a lobby or match ends).
  • Live game messages (aims, shots and other moves) are relayed in real time and are not stored. Pen Fight has no chat.
  • Match results: room code, desk, winner, each player’s name, score, knockouts and pen.

On your device

The game keeps your save data, settings and a random player ID in your browser’s or the app’s local storage. An offline profile is stored only on your device.

2. How we use it

  • Account management: signing you in and keeping your progress across devices.
  • App functionality: saving progress, online rooms and matches, the leaderboard and daily rewards.
  • Security: access rules make sure only you can change your own profile, rooms and match reports.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or marketing, and we do not use an advertising ID.

3. What other players can see

Your gamer name, profile photo (if you have one), avatar colour, level, trophies, coins and match stats can appear on the public leaderboard, in online rooms and in match results. Your email address is never visible to other players.

4. Sharing and service providers

We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties. We use these service providers (processors) to run the game, and they handle data only on our behalf:

ProviderWhat for
SupabaseDatabase, sign-in (authentication), real-time online play and hosting of game data.
VercelHosting the Pen Fight website. Like any web host it processes technical request data (such as IP address) to deliver and protect the site.
Google“Sign in with Google”. Google’s own privacy policy applies to your Google account.

We may disclose information if required by law.

5. Security

  • All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Database access rules (row-level security) only let you change your own data, and email addresses are not readable through the public game API.

6. How long we keep data

  • Account and progress: for as long as your account exists.
  • Online rooms: deleted automatically shortly after they become inactive.
  • Match results: kept as game history. When you delete your account they are anonymised (your name becomes “Deleted player” and your account ID is removed).
  • Backups: deleted data may remain in our hosting provider’s routine backups for a limited time until those backups expire.

7. Deleting your data

  • In the app: open Settings, find the Account panel, tap Delete account, type DELETE and confirm. Your account and progress are deleted immediately.
  • On the web: see how to delete your Pen Fight account, including how to ask by email.
  • Offline profile: use Settings → Reset progress, or uninstall the app / clear the site’s data.

You can also change your gamer name in the game at any time, and ask us for a copy of your data by email.

8. Children

Pen Fight is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has signed in, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the effective date above, and tell you in the game for significant changes.
