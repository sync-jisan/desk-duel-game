Delete your Pen Fight account

Pen Fight · Last updated 22 October 2026

Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Pen Fight and sign in with the Google account you want to delete.
  2. On the main menu, tap the Settings (gear) button.
  3. In the Account panel, tap Delete account.
  4. Read what will be deleted, type DELETE and tap Delete forever.
  5. Your account is deleted straight away. You are signed out, and the game’s data on that device is cleared.

Playing with an offline profile? It has no account and is stored only on your device. Wipe it with Settings → Reset progress, or by uninstalling the app or clearing the site’s data.

What happens to your data

Deleted

  • Your account and its Google sign-in link (name, email, profile photo, account ID)
  • Your profile: gamer name, avatar, coins, XP, level, trophies
  • Pens and desks you own, career progress, achievements
  • Match statistics and your leaderboard entry
  • Online rooms you are hosting

Kept, anonymised

  • Past match results, so other players’ history stays correct. Your name is replaced with “Deleted player” and your account ID is removed, so they are no longer linked to you.

Deleted data may remain in our hosting provider’s routine backups for a limited time until those backups expire. Deleted progress cannot be restored.

Can’t open the app? Ask by email

Email CONTACT_EMAIL from the Gmail address you use to sign in, with the subject “Delete my Pen Fight account”. We will confirm the request and delete your account and data as described above within 30 days.

Email a deletion request

You can also remove Pen Fight’s access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account’s Security → Third-party connections page. This stops future sign-ins but does not delete your game data, so please also delete your account as shown above.
