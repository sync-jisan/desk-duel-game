## Full description (max 4000)

```
Flick. Smash. Rule the desk.

Pen Fight is a top-down physics battle where every flick counts. Aim, pull back and launch your pen to knock rival pens off the edge of the desk. Hit too hard and you might sail off with them. The last pen standing takes the round.

REAL DESK PHYSICS
Every pen has its own weight, grip, bounce and spin. Light pens zip across the surface. Heavy metal pens hit like a truck and are hard to move. Bank shots off obstacles, set up chain reactions and stop a hair from the edge.

11 PENS, 5 WEIGHT CLASSES
Collect 11 pens inspired by famous real-world classics: a trusty ballpoint, a wooden HB pencil, a 4-colour clicker, a gel pen, a highlighter, a permanent marker, a fountain pen and solid metal and brass heavyweights. Each one handles differently, from Featherweight up to Titan.

7 DESKS TO FIGHT ON
Classroom oak, a library table with book stacks to hide behind, a tight exam paper, a slick glass office, a grippy art-room mat, a mahogany study and polished boardroom marble. Every surface changes how far your pen slides.

CAREER: 5 LEAGUES, 20 RIVALS
Climb from the Rookie Desk to Titan Tower. Each league is a weight class with its own home desk, three rivals and a boss waiting at the end. Beat the final boss to become the Desk Battle Champion.

QUICK MATCH
Jump straight into a game against the CPU on Easy, Medium, Hard or Expert. Choose your pen, your desk and how many rounds it takes to win.

PARTY MODE
Play with 2 or 3 players on one device. Take turns passing the phone around, or fill a seat with a CPU rival.

ONLINE WITH FRIENDS
Create a room, share the code and battle your friends online, up to 3 players per room.

LEADERBOARD, ACHIEVEMENTS & DAILY REWARDS
Climb the global leaderboard, unlock achievements like Double Trouble and Chain Reaction, and come back every day to build your 7-day reward streak. Earn coins from wins, achievements and daily rewards to unlock new pens and desks.

Easy to learn, satisfying to master. Grab a pen and take the desk!
```

## Screenshots: what Google needs

- **Phone:** 2 to 8 screenshots (at least 2 are required to publish). JPEG or 24-bit PNG, **no transparency**.
- **Dimensions:** shortest side **at least 320 px**, longest side **at most 3840 px**. The long side can't be more than 2× the short side, so use **9:16 portrait** (e.g. **1080×1920**) or 16:9 landscape (1920×1080).
- To be eligible for Play's promotional placements and recommendations, provide **at least 4 screenshots at 1080 px or more** on the short side (1080×1920 portrait is ideal).
- 7-inch and 10-inch tablet screenshots are optional but improve the tablet listing (same format rules).
- Suggested set: (1) Career league map, (2) a mid-flick aim shot on the Classroom Desk, (3) the impact moment with a pen flying off the edge, (4) the pen collection or shop, (5) Party mode setup with 3 players, (6) an online room lobby, (7) the leaderboard, (8) the daily reward streak.
